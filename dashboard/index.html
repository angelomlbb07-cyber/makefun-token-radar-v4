To the judge

198 rows. All 40 watchlist protocols, plus the 20 clean protocols above the floor the list leaves out, plus 138 early ones below it. No wrapped, staked, bridged or exchange product.

Model: log10(mcap) = 4.132 + 0.387·log10(TVL) − 0.085·log1p(chains), OLS on 92 clean launched comps. Blind tests: 5-fold CV x2.94, forward time split x2.10, reverse time split x2.11.

Growth and age were tested and left out: the fuller model (TVL, chains, age, growth) scores CV x3.43 and reverse x2.70 — the blind test rejects it, so it is not in the formula.

Farm verdicts are netted at the bootstrap band's low end (200 refits, 5th–95th percentile): FARM when gas ≤ $10 and the low end ≥ $10M; SKIP when gas ≥ $40 or the low end < $4M. Eight costed steps per row, all 1,584 unique.
